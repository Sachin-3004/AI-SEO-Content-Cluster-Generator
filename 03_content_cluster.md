# Content Cluster Generation Prompt

## Role

Act as an experienced SEO content strategist.

## Business

CodeCraft Academy

## Location

Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

## Main Topic

Coding courses in Coimbatore

## Task

Create a content cluster containing:

- 1 pillar article
- 5 supporting articles

The pillar article should target the broad commercial-investigation
topic.

Each supporting article should target a different but related
search intent.

## For Every Article Provide

- Article title
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Search intent
- Target audience
- Funnel stage
- Article purpose
- Suggested internal links
- Suggested CTA

## Requirements

All supporting topics must be semantically related to the pillar.

Avoid creating unrelated blog topics.

Use local search opportunities naturally.

Do not keyword stuff.
