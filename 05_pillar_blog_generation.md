# Pillar Blog Generation Prompt

## Role

Act as an expert SEO content writer.

## Business

CodeCraft Academy

## Location

Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

## Topic

Coding Courses in Coimbatore:
A Beginner's Guide to Choosing the Right Course

## Primary Keyword

coding courses in Coimbatore

## Search Intent

Commercial investigation.

## Target Audience

Students, beginners, graduates, job seekers and career switchers.

## Task

Write a helpful long-form SEO article using the approved H1-H3 outline.

## Content Requirements

The article should:

- Answer the reader's main questions
- Explain different types of coding courses
- Help beginners understand their options
- Discuss factors to consider before choosing a course
- Include practical-learning considerations
- Include local context about Coimbatore
- Use the primary keyword naturally
- Use related keywords naturally
- Include FAQs
- Include a clear conclusion
- Include a soft CTA for CodeCraft Academy

## Writing Style

- Human-readable
- Clear
- Helpful
- Professional
- Beginner-friendly
- Short paragraphs
- Avoid unnecessary jargon

## SEO Rules

- Do not keyword stuff
- Do not write for search engines at the expense of readers
- Do not make unsupported claims
- Do not invent statistics
- Do not guarantee rankings
- Do not claim that the business is the "best" unless supported by
  independently verifiable evidence

## Internal Linking

Include contextual internal-link suggestions for supporting articles.

## Output

Return:

1. SEO title
2. Suggested meta description
3. H1-H3 article
4. Internal linking suggestions
5. CTA
6. FAQs
